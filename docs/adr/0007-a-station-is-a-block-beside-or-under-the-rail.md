# A Station is a block beside or under the Rail

Factorio's train stop is an entity beside the track, and a train stops with its front at it. Railworks' Rail is raised and a car rides on top of it (ADR 0006), so a Station could have hung off a Pylon, sat on top of an Arm, or been a block somewhere near a Span. It is a block beside the Rail or under it: its centre within 1.25 blocks of the Rail's line, which takes the block on either side of the Rail at the car's floor or one lower, and the block under it, and not the Rail's own block or anything in the Clearance, which a passing car would hit. It serves the one Span nearest it, between two as near the one whose ends sort first, and a car whose Place is within a block either way of the point of that Span nearest the Station, its foot, is by it. A Train at rest with any of its cars by a Station is stopped at it as a whole, as a Factorio train is at its stop whichever wagon is beside it, so every Cargo car of it loads and unloads there, wherever along the Train it stands (#25: a Train first had to stop with the Cargo car itself by the Station, and a player who stopped it with the Locomotive there found nothing to load). Those rules are `rail/Station` and `Stations.stoppedAt`, in the model, so Schedules (#26) and loading (#25) ask the same question, with a Train's chunks loaded or not (ADR 0004).

Nothing ties a Station to its Span when it is placed: which Span it serves is asked afresh of the Spans each time, so a Span strung, cut or moved by a stacked segment needs no bookkeeping, and a Station whose Span is cut serves nothing until one is strung by it again. The level keeps every Station's block and name in its saved data beside the Spans, so a Schedule finds the Stations of a name with their chunks unloaded; each Station block keeps its name too, which its menu and Jade read.

The placement is chosen for the hoppers of #25. NeoForge's hopper finds an entity that is a `Container`, or failing that one with an item capability, in the block it pushes into or pulls from, and goes through a `WorldlyContainer`'s sides; the Cargo car is one, its sides open only while it loads, and gives the capability on the same rule for mods that look for it, and the car straddles the Rail in the block above it. A hopper beside the car at its floor, pointing into it, loads it; a hopper under the Rail, between the Pylons, pulls from the car above it and unloads it (`rail/HopperSpots`). Nothing in the Rail's row has a face to click to point a hopper sideways at the car, least of all on a raised line, so sneak-using a hopper at a Cargo car stopped at a Station sets it in the spot on the player's side, pointing in, standing in the air; using a Cargo car otherwise opens its cargo, and nobody rides one, so the sneak is free for it. (A hopper over the roof pointing down was tried first, but loading from the side is what a player looks for.) While a car is stopped at a Station, the client draws the spots of the one Cargo car under the crosshair, the car a sneak-used hopper would go beside, and no other car's, so a long Train at a Station is not a field of boxes. A car loads only on a Straight leg, every Span under its body in line through both its Arms or ending there, and along x or z (`rail/StraightLeg`): on a bend the Rail curves (#19) and a block beside the car is in its path, and on a diagonal the whole-block spots beside the car are in the Rail's path a block on, so a car leaving would run through its hopper. A Station is placed only by a Straight leg: every Span its stretch touches, over a Pylon onto the Span beyond too, straight and along x or z (`StraightLeg.at`), so a Station by a bend or a diagonal, or close enough to a Pylon to reach a bent Span past it, is refused with the Placement Preview red. Only the stretch is asked, not the body of a car at its edge, so a bend just past it lets the Station place while a car stopped at that edge will not load; the per-car check holds there. Since a Span strung at an Arm later can still bend a stretch that was straight, loading is asked of each car too, and Jade on a Station whose stretch has been bent says that a Cargo car will not load there. A Station beside the Rail at the car's floor takes one of the two loading spots and leaves the other, and one a block lower or under the Rail takes neither, or the unloading spot. What it gives up is a Station on a Pylon, and a Station reached across a gap: a Station is always next to the Rail it serves. The design is 5thlayer/railworks#24.
