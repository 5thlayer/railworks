# A Station is a block beside or under the Rail

Factorio's train stop is an entity beside the track, and a train stops with its front at it. Railworks' Rail is raised and a car rides on top of it (ADR 0006), so a Station could have hung off a Pylon, sat on top of an Arm, or been a block somewhere near a Span. It is a block beside the Rail or under it: its centre within 1.25 blocks of the Rail's line, which takes the block on either side of the Rail at the car's floor or one lower, and the block under it, and not the Rail's own block or anything in the Clearance, which a passing car would hit. It serves the one Span nearest it, between two as near the one whose ends sort first, and a car whose Place is within a block either way of the point of that Span nearest the Station, its foot, is at it. That rule is `rail/Station`, in the model, so Schedules (#26) and loading (#25) ask the same question, with a Train's chunks loaded or not (ADR 0004).

Nothing ties a Station to its Span when it is placed: which Span it serves is asked afresh of the Spans each time, so a Span strung, cut or moved by a stacked segment needs no bookkeeping, and a Station whose Span is cut serves nothing until one is strung by it again. The level keeps every Station's block and name in its saved data beside the Spans, so a Schedule finds the Stations of a name with their chunks unloaded; each Station block keeps its name too, which its menu and Jade read.

The placement is chosen for the hoppers of #25. NeoForge's hopper finds an entity's item capability in the block it pushes into or pulls from, and the car straddles the Rail in the block above it. A hopper beside the car at its floor, pointing into it, would load it, but nothing there has a face to click to point a hopper sideways into the Rail's row, so #25 loads from a hopper over the car's roof pointing down, which a hopper placed against the underside of a block is (`rail/HopperSpots`); a hopper under the Rail, between the Pylons, pulls from the car above it and unloads it. While a car is stopped at a Station, the client draws both spots. A Station takes none of those blocks unless it is the one under the Rail, so a Station beside the Rail leaves both free, and one under the Rail leaves the roof's. What it gives up is a Station on a Pylon, and a Station reached across a gap: a Station is always next to the Rail it serves. The design is 5thlayer/railworks#24.
