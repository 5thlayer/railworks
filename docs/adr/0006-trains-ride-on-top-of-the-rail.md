# Trains ride on top of the Rail

Railworks was first designed as a suspended monorail, after the SAFEGE lines and Dortmund's H-Bahn: a car hanging under an overhead Rail strung from the side Arm of each Γ Pylon. It changed before any release to a straddle-beam monorail, after the ALWEG lines: the Rail is an inverted T resting on top of each Pylon, and a car rides on it, straddling the web. The look was the reason, since a car riding its beam reads as a monorail at a glance. The geometry agreed: a hanging car needs every Arm offset to one side of its column so the car passes beside it, a facing on every Arm and every rule measured from its tip, while an Arm on top of the column needs neither. What it gives up is the hanger Pylon under a ceiling or a bridge, and the space under the Rail as the Train's. Clearance is now the space above a Span and down both sides of the web, and the space below is free to build in. Getting off a raised car would be the fall it was under a suspended one, so a passenger is put down on the first floor beside or below the car, at any height, or stays aboard where there is none (#17). The design is 5thlayer/railworks#1.
