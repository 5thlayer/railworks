# The Rail carries no energy

> Amended in #9: the Electric Locomotive takes no FE through a capability on itself, since one on a car's view would charge it only in view and only by a cable that could reach a moving entity. A **Charger**, a block beside a Station's platform joining it as a hatch does, takes FE from any mod's cable and charges the Electric Locomotive of a Train at rest beside it, manual or automatic, its charge and the Charger's buffer kept in the level's record (ADR 0008). The Locomotive's view exposes its charge read-only, for display. The Burner Generator makes FE from fuel only beside itself, so Railworks plays alone; it carries nothing over distance, and with Wireworks in a pack, Wireworks' generators and poles power a Charger the same way.

Energy over distance is Wireworks' capability (FactoryWorks ADR-0017), and a Rail that powered its Trains would make every line a free power line beside it. So the Rail carries nothing: the Burner Locomotive carries its own fuel, and the Electric Locomotive takes FE through NeoForge's energy capability only while stopped at a Station, from whatever powers that Station. The design is 5thlayer/railworks#1.
