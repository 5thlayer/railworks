# A Train in unloaded chunks keeps moving as an abstract Train

> Amended in #6: a car's view is never saved with its chunk. The level spawns a car's view where its Train puts it once that chunk is loaded and ticking, and takes it away, never breaking it, when the chunk unloads, so a Train is one form or the other and a reloaded chunk brings back no stale car. A car entity found in a saved chunk, from a world saved before #6, is adopted once and then dropped from the chunk, its cargo and fuel moved into the level's record (ADR 0008). Every Train ticks from the level's tick, in view or not, and a view never runs its Train but only follows it, striking what is in its path; so a Train half in loaded chunks is one Train, ticked once, and an abstract Train is simply one with none of its cars in view. A view a player rides is never taken away by its chunk's state: a Locomotive can outrun chunk loading, and the Train moves its rider's view along with it until the chunk catches up. A mob riding a car whose view is taken away rides on in the level's record and reappears seated with the view. An abstract Train whose Span is cut stands where it is, off the Rail and no longer ticking, and each of its cars comes off the Rail, dropping itself and what it holds, as its chunk loads, so nothing is dropped into an unloaded chunk and no car is lost.

> Amended by ADR 0008: Trains take jobs from hatches, not Schedules, so an abstract Train keeps its job and its claims where this said its Schedule.

A Train whose chunks unload becomes an abstract Train: its cars, cargo, fuel, job and claims are held by the rail network model, which advances it along the stored Spans on the same tick it advances every other Train. When its chunks load again it reappears as entities where the model says it is. Trains never force-load chunks, so a network costs a server no chunk-loading. This works only because a Train never checks for blocks while it moves (only a loaded Train meets players and mobs): a Span's Clearance is guaranteed when the Span is strung, and a block placed into it later is refused, so the model needs no blocks to know the way is free. The alternatives were freezing Trains outside loaded chunks, which stops a distant outpost line, and chunk-loading their path, which costs the server. The design is 5thlayer/railworks#1.
