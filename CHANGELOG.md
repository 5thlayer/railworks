# Changelog

Written for Consumers: what a mod building against Railworks can use, or will see change. A published version never changes: a fix is the next patch (`docs/agents/releases.md`).

## Unreleased
- Pylons and Rail: a Pylon crafts from iron and stacks into a column, one Pylon a segment, its Arm on the top segment. Using Rail on one Arm and then another strings a Span costing `ceil(length)` Rail, up to 32 blocks (`spanLengthLimit` in `railworks-server.toml`), at most two to an Arm. Repeating it cuts the Span; breaking a Pylon cuts its Spans, and either refunds the Rail. Spans are saved with the level and a client is sent those in the chunks it loads.
- The Pylon shows Groundworks' Placement Preview, including the segment it would stack onto a column. Placing a new column strings one Span to the nearest Pylon in reach whose Arm has a Span to spare, for Rail from the placer's inventory (creative players too); the preview draws it, faded. Stacking a segment, sneaking, or too little Rail strings nothing.
- A Rail holding the first end of a Span shows a slack Rail from that Arm to the hand, moving to the Pylon aimed at: green where the click would string, orange where it would cut (the Span itself stops drawing) and red where it would be refused.
- The Rail is drawn as an inverted T, its flange resting on the Pylon tops, and a car rides on top of it, straddling the web (ADR 0006).
- Grade and bend limits: a Span climbing steeper than 25% (`spanGradeLimitPercent` in `railworks-server.toml`), or bending through an Arm more than 45 degrees off straight against the Span already there (`spanBendLimit`), is refused and spends nothing, whether strung by hand or by a placed Pylon, which strings to the nearest Arm within both limits instead. The held Rail's slack shows both refusals red, with an action-bar line saying why; the Pylon's Placement Preview draws the Span the limits refused red when it would string nothing. The bend rule is `SpanRules.tooSharp`, judging any two Spans through an Arm. Jade on a Pylon shows the length of each Span at its Arm and, while Rail holds a pending Span, why the Span to that Arm would be refused.
- Burner Locomotive: crafts from iron and a furnace, and a Locomotive in the hand shows Groundworks' Placement Preview of the car riding the Span aimed along (refused off a Span, where the car would sit in a block above the Span, or on a Span another Locomotive holds); using it sets it on the Rail facing the way the player looks. Sneaking at the car, or the inventory key while riding it, opens its menu: the fuel slot (furnace fuel only) over the player's inventory, shift-click moving fuel either way. Fuel in hand still fills the slot, using it otherwise boards it, and sneaking gets off. Forward accelerates the way it faces; back brakes and then reverses, as in Factorio's manual driving. It burns 600 kW of fuel at 2,500 J per burn tick (`joules_per_burn_tick`, as Wireworks' Boiler: coal is 4 MJ), runs on through Arms, and stops at a line's end and when its fuel runs out. A Locomotive is a car of a Train in the rail network model (`rail/Train`, an ordered chain of `rail/Car`s carrying their mass, their fuelled Locomotives' pull and the Spans they stand on), with the entity as its car's view; its top speed (25 blocks a second; Factorio's 72 is allowed), acceleration, braking and friction are in `railworks-server.toml`. Stacking a segment onto a strung column keeps a Locomotive on its Span. Jade shows its fuel and speed.
- Stations: a Station crafts from iron, a Rail and a sign, sits in the Works tab and places in the row beside the Rail, two blocks off its line in the block a Cargo car's chests sit in, the second above the Rail's underside (`Station.ROW_OFF`, ADRs 0007 and 0010), refused anywhere else, or by Rail that is not a Straight leg. Using it opens its menu, where it is named, and several may share a name. The name is kept with the block, and the level keeps every Station's block and name (`LevelStations`) so a Train can be driven to one unloaded. Whether a car's Place is at a Station, within a block either way of the point of the nearest Span by it, is `Station.serves` and `Stations.at` in the rail network model. Jade shows a Station's name, and when it stands by no Span.
- Cargo cars: a Cargo car crafts from iron round a chest (`#c:chests/wooden`) and holds a double chest's 54 slots, its menu (vanilla's six-row chest) opening when it is used, sneaking or not. Placing a Cargo car or a Locomotive beside an end of a Train at rest couples it into that Train, facing the way the player looks, so a Train can carry a Locomotive facing out at each end; the Placement Preview draws it there. Shears on a car part its Train at the coupling on the side used, while it stands still. Every car adds weight: a Train accelerates by its fuelled Locomotives' pull over its whole mass, and brakes by its Locomotives' brakes over it, so a Cargo car (1,000 kg, a Locomotive's half) stretches both. A driver drives the Train the way their own Locomotive faces, and the Locomotives facing that way pull. A Locomotive is boarded by using it; a player leading a mob puts the mob in, and a lead used on it leads its mob out. Nobody rides a Cargo car, player or mob. The level now holds its Trains (`rail/Trains`, saved with the Spans), each car's entity keeping only its car's id; a Train holds every Span its cars' bodies stand on, and stops at the Arm of a Span another Train holds. Breaking a car closes its Train up without it and drops what it held; a cut Span drops every car of the Train on it.
- The Rail curves through each Pylon: a Span is drawn as a smooth curve between its two Arms, at each Arm along the bisector of the Spans meeting there and, at a line's end, heading straight at the other Arm; the Pylon's Placement Preview draws the curve the Span would take. Every car rides the curve, its position, heading, pitch and its rider's seat following it, with no jump from one Span onto the next, and a car in the hand aims at the curve. The curve is `rail/RailCurve` (`RailNetwork.curve(span)`, `RailCurve.between` for a Span not yet strung, `Place.pose`), which the renderer and the riding code both ask; `Place.point()` became `Place.point(network)`. Rail cost, the length limit and how far a Train runs along a Span stay the straight distance between the Arms.
- Stopped at a Station: a Train at rest with its leading car at a Station, arriving its serving way, is stopped at it (`Stations.stoppedAt` in the rail network model). A Station is refused, the Placement Preview red, unless every Span its stretch touches is a Straight leg, in line at both Arms (or ending there) and running along x or z, a stretch reaching over a Pylon included (`rail/StraightLeg`); Jade on a Station whose stretch a Span strung later has bent says so. A Cargo car is filled and emptied by hand through its menu only: it is no `Container` and gives no item capability, so no hopper, feeder or pipe reaches its cargo, at a Station or anywhere else, and sneak-using a hopper at it opens its menu. Jade still lists what it holds, as icons with their counts, from a Railworks Jade plugin that reads the car's cargo rather than a `Container`. A Station's hatches are to load and unload it unattended (ADR 0008).
- The cars are redrawn as boxy cars straddling the Rail: each is a shell over skirts down both sides of the Rail's web, with a full-length roof, a dark window band along each side running on from car to car, and a darker gangway door on each end that couples. The Locomotive's front is an open cab, a dark frame of roof edge, corner pillars and a low dash over a deep red nose with two headlights, with an open window in each side of the cab, and its rider now sits in the cab, seeing the line ahead past the frame and out of either side. The Cargo car is closed, with vanilla chests set into its sides. The boxes both are drawn from, and the seat, are `rail/CarBody`.
- Sounds: setting a Locomotive or a Cargo car on the Rail, or coupling one into a Train, plays an iron block's place sound pitched down at the car, heard by the placer and those nearby; a refused placement plays none. Opening a Cargo car's cargo plays a chest's open sound and closing it the close sound, for the first to open it and the last to close it, with the container game events a chest boat sends.
- Driving to a Station: the rail network model's `rail/Driver` takes a Train to a Station and stops it with its leading car there: on along its Line the nearer way a Locomotive with fuel in its buffer or its slot faces, round a closed loop or, with a Locomotive facing out at each end, back along a line, braking in time; where the Station is out of reach it holds where it is. `Place.distanceTo` measures along the Line through the Arms. It drives automatic Trains to their jobs' Stations, below, with `new Driver(station, way)` keeping to one way so the cars stand on the car spots the job counts on; `Driver.lost()` says the Station is out of reach. A Train saved with an earlier build's Schedule loads, driven by hand. The network version is now 3.
- Stations face by drive side: a Station serves only the Trains that have it on their drive side, the right unless `driveSide` in `railworks-server.toml` is `LEFT`, as a Factorio train stop does, and lets the others pass (`Station.servingEnd`, `rail/DriveSide`). Its top shows an arrow pointing the way the Trains it serves head, and Jade says it ("Serves Trains heading north"). A Station whose block or platform would overlap another Station's is refused, the Placement Preview red and the action bar naming the other Station. A Station's platform is the Straight leg behind it on its serving way: the leading car's place at the Station and up to four car spots of five blocks each behind it (`platformCars`, capped at 4), the first the car coupled behind the leading car, ending where the leg ends at a bend or a line's end, a Station by the end Arm keeping its spots back from there as the leading car's front stands over the Arm (`rail/Platform`, with which spot a block in the row beside the Rail is beside, and whether two platforms overlap). The Driver stops a Train only arriving a Station's serving way, its leading car's front at the Station block's front edge, and a Train is stopped at a Station only so. A Train left at rest within a Station's stretch, nobody driving it, eases onto the Station's mark at a block a second, so its cars stand on the car spots (`Stations.settle`).
- Car lengths: cars couple their lengths apart, middle to middle, on straight and curved Rail, each a whole number of blocks long coupler to coupler, so a stopped Train's cars line up with the blocks; a car's body falls 0.2 short of its length (`CarBody.GAP`), leaving that gap between coupled bodies. A Locomotive's and a Cargo car's lengths are below (#62).
- Hatches: a hatch crafts from iron ingots round a chest (`#c:chests/wooden`) and a hopper, sits in the Works tab and places only beside a car spot of a Station's platform, in the row a Station stands in either side of the Rail, two off it at a Cargo car's chests' height, joining that Station; elsewhere it is refused, the Placement Preview red (`RailworksRefusal.HATCH_OFF_PLATFORM`). While one is held, the free blocks beside the car spots of the platforms near are outlined. It is a provider (the recipe's item) or a requester, each its own item: sneak-used in the air the stack switches, and an empty-hand sneak-use switches a placed hatch, keeping what it holds. Using a requester with an item asks for a stack more of that kind, up to its slots, another kind replacing the request with one stack; an empty hand clears it, and takes a provider's first stack. It holds `hatchSlots` (27) slots, set in `railworks-server.toml`, and has no owner. The level keeps every hatch's mode, request and contents in saved data beside the Stations (`LevelHatches`), and the block's item handler (`Capabilities.Item.BLOCK`, every side) is that record, so hoppers and pipes fill and empty it; a hatch set by a command registers itself as it loads, and breaking one drops what it holds. Which Station a hatch joins is asked afresh (`Platform.joined`, with `Platform.besideBlocks` and `rail/HatchRequest` in the rail network model), so one whose Station is broken or whose platform is bent away is a plain container until a platform covers it again. Jade on a hatch shows its mode, request and contents and the Station and car spot it joins, or that it joins none; Jade on a Station shows how many hatches joined it. Automatic Trains trade with hatches, below.
- Automatic Trains run jobs (ADR 0008): the Locomotive's menu has an Automatic toggle for its Train, saved with it (`Train.automatic`); a driver pressing forward or back switches it to manual, a Train coupled to keeps its mode and the Trains it parts into are manual. The rail network model's `rail/Logistics`, ticked with the level and given a `rail/Goods` view of what hatches and Cargo cars hold, plans once a second: requesters in the order they were placed, each with need left (its request less what it holds and what is on its way), get the nearest provider Station offering that item, never their own, and the nearest free automatic Train (no job, empty Cargo cars) that reaches the provider arriving its serving way and the requester after it takes the job, driven there by `rail/Driver`. A job carries a load for every car spot where the requester Station's hatch asks for what the provider Station's hatch offers, so a Train carries a different item in each car, and two in one car where two hatch pairs share a spot; each load is the least of the need, the provider's unclaimed stock and the car's room, claimed against both hatches. At a Station each hatch moves one stack every `hatchTicksPerStack` (20) ticks with the car at its spot, only for an automatic Train on a job there, so a Train driven by hand is never emptied; a Train leaves when every load of the stop is moved, or after `stallSeconds` (5) with nothing moving, a part load from a provider delivered as it is and an empty one dropping its job. An automatic Train with no job waits where it is. The menu and Jade on a Locomotive show its status line: "Manual", "Waiting for a job", "To X to load", "Loading at X", "To X to unload: 6 stacks of iron ore, 2 of coal", "Unloading at X" or "Cannot reach X". Jobs are not saved: a Train reloaded mid-job is free again once its cars are empty. A Cargo car's cargo is its entity's, so a hatch trades only with a car in view.
- Claims break, and leftovers are delivered (#38): a Train switched to manual, coupled, parted, broken up or derailed drops its job and every claim, its cars keeping what they hold. A hatch broken or switched in mode drops the loads claimed against it: one gone from a provider keeps what it took, one gone from a requester leaves what it took in its car as a leftover, and a job with no loads left is cancelled. Where a request changed on the way, a Train loads and unloads no more than the requester now wants, the rest a leftover, and a job that can no longer reach its requester is given up, what it carries a leftover. An automatic Train holding anything, leftovers or cargo loaded by hand, takes only delivery jobs, with no provider: to the nearest requester Station, either way it pulls, whose hatch at a car's spot asks for what that car holds. With none it waits where it is, its status line "Holding 2 stacks of iron ore, nowhere to deliver", counted in stacks as "To X to unload" is (`Logistics.Kind.HOLDING`). `rail/Goods` gains `contents(CargoCar)`, what a car holds item by item.
- Gallery pack: the jar carries **Railworks Gallery** (`mod/railworks:gallery_pack`), a datapack of functions only, off until a player turns it on in the Data Packs screen or with `/datapack enable` (libworks ADR 0010). `/function railworks:gallery/build` builds a scene of each feature, one plot behind another going north, each 40 by 15 blocks with a sign naming it that rebuilds it when clicked: `pylons`, `limits` (the grade and bend limits, with an unstrung Pylon past each), `curves`, `locomotive` (fuelled, to drive), `coupling` (a Locomotive and two Cargo cars, with a spare of each and shears), `stations` (one on each side of the Rail), `logistics` (an automatic Train of a Locomotive at each end shuttling iron ore from a provider hatch to a requester emptied into a chest), `two_loads` (two Cargo cars, each carrying its own item) and `flyover` (a high line crossing over a low one without joining, a fuelled Locomotive on each, #49); `/function railworks:gallery/<scene>` builds one. The libworks runtime it needs is 0.1.12, up from 0.1.8.
- Operator commands, which the Gallery pack builds with: `/railworks span <from> <to>` strings a Span between the Arms of the Pylon columns at the two positions for no Rail, refused as a player would be past the length, grade and bend limits or at a full Arm; `/railworks car (locomotive|cargo_car) <pos> <yaw>` sets a car on the Rail where a car riding it stands in the block `pos`, facing `yaw`, as the item does, coupled into a Train at rest beside whose end it lands and refused where the item would be, with the item's place sound. `/railworks hatch <pos> provider <item> <stacks>` puts so many full stacks in a provider hatch, and `requester` has a requester ask for that many; `/railworks automatic <locomotive>` switches its Train to automatic, as the menu's toggle does. A refusal fails the command. `CarPlacement.plan` takes a `CarPlacement.Sight`, a player's or a command's line of sight. Fuel and cargo go in by `data merge entity` (`cab:[...]`, `cargo:{Items:[...]}`).
- Hatches show the item they are set up for (#45): a requester its request's item, a provider its first stack, drawn flat on each of the hatch's four sides as an item frame holds one, half an item's size and lit as the block in front of that side; an empty provider and a requester asking for nothing show nothing. `HatchBlockEntity.shown()` is worked out from the hatch's record (`Hatches.Hatch.shown()`) each server tick, so it follows a hopper, a Train, a player, a mode switch or `/railworks hatch`, and a change made with its chunk unloaded once it loads; it is sent to watchers in the block's update tag, alone, only as it changes, and never saved.
- Clearance (#20, #75): a Span keeps clear the space a car straddling it passes through, down both sides of its web, along its curve: its body, a rigid box 4.8 long, two blocks across and three up from the Rail, its middle at each spot along the Span and turned and pitched the way the curve runs there (`Clearance.BODY_LENGTH`). So a straight Span's is three blocks across and reaches two blocks past each Arm, where a car's middle may stand, and a bent Span's is wider where a car's ends swing out of the curve and its middle cuts in, so a car on a bend at the bend limit never reaches into a block the Clearance calls clear, and a Span is refused and a block placed refused there. A Span running on in line into a bent Span shorter than `Clearance.SWING_CLEAR_LENGTH` (4) is no Straight leg (`StraightLeg.is`), since a car swinging round that bend reaches the row beside it, so a platform ends a Span short of such a bend, and a Station placed by it is refused (`rail/Clearance`, which asks the world only which blocks are filled through `Clearance.Blocks`; a block is filled where it has a collision shape). A Span whose Clearance a block fills is refused, by hand, by a placed Pylon (which strings to the nearest Arm whose Span is clear instead) and by `/railworks span`, and spends nothing, as is one that would bend the Span already at either Arm into a block (`Stringing.Result.BLOCKED`, the first block in the way in `Stringing.Outcome.blocked`). The held Rail's slack and the Pylon's Placement Preview show it red, and the action bar and Jade name the block in the way. A block placed into a strung Span's Clearance is refused on both sides, its item kept; a Pylon segment stacked on the Span's own Arm is not in its way, and the row two off a Straight leg where Stations, hatches and Chargers stand is clear. Another Span's Rail is no block, so one line passes over or under another at any height (ADR 0002). A car's placement refusal where it would sit in a block (`RailworksRefusal.BLOCKED_CAR`) asks the same body (`Clearance.car`, which takes a `RailCurve.Pose`). `Stringing.decide`, `click` and `tintFor` and `AutoStringing.choose` and `refused` take a `Clearance.Blocks`, `SpanGesture.reason` takes the `Stringing.Outcome`, and `RailCurve` gains `a()`, `b()` and `strung`.
- Train collision (#21): a moving car strikes every player and mob in the space its box swept that tick, hurting it by how fast it runs, 0.8 health for each block a second (20, an unarmoured player's whole health, at the default top speed), with the `railworks:train` damage type ("was struck by Burner Locomotive"), and pushing it aside, off the Rail to the side it stands on, carried on at the car's speed and lifted a little (`rail/Impact`). A Train at rest strikes nothing, and whoever rides a car, a Locomotive's driver or a mob led in, is never struck.
- Clearance kept (#20): a Pylon segment stacked on an Arm is refused, the Placement Preview red and the action bar saying why, where it would raise one of the Arm's Spans past the length, grade or bend limit or into a block, or would itself stand in another Span's Clearance (`RailworksRefusal.RAISES_SPAN`, the rule `Stringing.raise`, ADR 0009); a sneaking player's segment is refused too. A segment set some other way, such as `/setblock`, that raises a Span past a rule cuts the Arm's Spans and drops their Rail. Past a player's placed block, a piston pushes or pulls no block into a strung Span's Clearance and puts no head there, water reaching lava (and lava flowing down into water) makes no block there, and a cactus or bamboo grows no higher than one. Still able to fill a Clearance: a falling block landing, a dispenser's shulker box, lava that turns to obsidian or cobblestone as it flows in beside water, and water freezing to ice or snow piling up in falling snow, which NeoForge reports to no event before the block is set; a tree or huge mushroom grown from a sapling, growth by bone meal, a melon or pumpkin set beside its stem and a chorus flower branching sideways, whose blocks are chosen after their events; and a bamboo sapling's first stalk.
- Refuelling at a Station (#53, ADR 0008 amended): a hatch now also places beside the leading car's place of a Station's platform (`Platform.LEADING`, a spot index of -1, drawn and shown in Jade as "leading car"), trading with a Cargo car standing there and pairing in jobs with the other Station's leading car's place. A held hatch outlines the free blocks beside the leading car's place in amber, apart from the car spots' cream (`PlatformsNear.freeSpots` now gives `FreeSpot`s, each saying whether it is the leading car's). A provider hatch fuels the Locomotive whose Engine room it stands beside, of an automatic Train at its Station, waiting or stopped there on its job, never a manual one nor one held there on its way elsewhere: a stack every hatch turn, up to the Locomotive's fuel slots, from what no job claims, and only fuel that leaves nothing behind when burnt, so lava buckets stay in the hatch. A Train on a job waits while fuel still goes in, and one that ran dry waiting is fuelled and takes jobs again. The Burner Locomotive has four fuel slots (`Locomotive.FUEL_SLOTS`), each a stack of one fuel, the fuels free to differ, burnt from the first that holds any; fuel in hand fills slots holding the same fuel first, then empty ones, and its menu shows the four. `Goods` gains `contents(hatch)`, `fuelRoom` and `refuel`, and `slotFuelled` becomes `slotsFuelled`. The Gallery's new `refuel` scene, laid after the flyover, sets a shuttle down dry at a Station with coal beside both its Locomotives.
- Cars' contents live in the level (#59, ADR 0008 amended in #6): a Cargo car's cargo and a Locomotive's four fuel slots are kept in the level's saved data beside the hatches (`LevelCarRecords`, `railworks:car_records`), by the car's id, whole stacks with their components, and the car's entity is only a view onto them: its menu, Jade, fuel in hand, burning fuel, a hatch's loading and unloading and a provider's refuelling all read and write the record. So the level's `Goods` now answer truly for a car out of view (what it holds, its room, whether it is empty, whether its fuel slots are fuelled), a hatch trades with a Cargo car and a provider fuels a Locomotive whose entity is unloaded, and a Locomotive out of view in a moving Train burns its slots' fuel too, except a fuel that leaves something behind, which waits for its view. A car's record follows it through coupling and uncoupling, and into another level with its entity, and goes when the car breaks, its view dropping what it held. The entities no longer save `cargo` or `cab`: one saved with them by an earlier build hands them to its record the first time it loads, once, and a car that already has a record keeps the record's. `data merge entity` with `cab:[...]` or `cargo:{Items:[...]}` still fills a car in the world, now setting its record, the slots it names and the rest emptied; `data get entity` no longer shows them. `LevelRails.goods(level)` gives the level's `Goods`.
- Logging out in a car (#56): a player logging out seated in a car is put off it first, as a sneak puts them off, so the car stays in the world in its Train and an automatic Train goes on with its job; vanilla otherwise takes a vehicle with one player aboard out of the world with them until they log back in. They log back in where they got off, not in the car, and a single-player world quit from a car's seat reloads with the player off it.
- Jade on a Locomotive (#60): its fuel slots show as item icons with their counts, in slot order, as a Cargo car's cargo does, in place of the `Slot:` line that named only the fuel it burns next. The `tooltip.railworks.locomotive.jade.slot` and `slot_empty` lang keys are gone, and `LocomotiveEntity.fuelItem()` now reads only on the server.
- A Train's status line names its cargo as icons (#61): heading to unload it reads `To X to unload` and with nowhere to deliver `Nowhere to deliver`, the items the Train carries following as item icons with their item counts (no longer stacks), in Jade and in the Locomotive's menu, which grows a strip taller for them; the first four items show and `+M` counts the rest. `Logistics.Cargo` now counts items (`count()`, was `stacks()`), and the lang keys `status.railworks.cargo.stack`, `stacks` and `separator` are gone, `status.railworks.cargo.more` now being `+%s`.
- Trains far away (#6, ADR 0004 amended): every Train ticks from the level's tick, in view or not, and the level shows a car, its view, only where its Train puts it in a chunk that ticks entities. An automatic Train with none of its cars in view, an abstract Train, runs its job on the same tick and the same Spans as every other, loading, unloading and refuelling at hatches with none of their chunks loaded, and a manual one coasts on and stops as it would in view; Trains never keep a chunk loaded. A car's entity is never saved with its chunk: the level spawns it as its place starts ticking entities and takes it away, breaking nothing, as it stops, and one found in a chunk saved by an earlier build hands over what it held, once, and goes, the level spawning its own. A view a player rides is never taken away, and the level ticks it, and its rider, where its chunk does not yet. A mob riding a car is kept in the car's record from when it gets in until it gets out (`CarRecords.Contents.rider()`), so it rides on when the car's view is taken away or the server stops, and is seated again as the view comes back. A Train whose Span is cut out of view stands where it was, and each of its cars comes off the Rail, dropping itself and what it holds, once its place ticks entities (`LevelRails.fallen()`), where before it was lost. A car carried into another level leaves its Train in the one it left. A car's view no longer runs its Train from `tick()`: `LevelRails.step(level, train)` ticks one Train as the level does, `LevelRails.standing(car)` says where a car stands in view or not, and `rail/Views` decides what the level shows. A Train whose Span is cut falls off the Rail as the Span is cut, by a Pylon breaking or a Rail click, rather than at the next tick, so a line strung again in the same tick is never held by it. The Gallery's new `far_away` scene, laid after `refuel`, sends a Train 240 blocks east past the loaded chunks to Mine and back with its ore to Smelter's chest by the plot, until Mine runs dry; it force-loads its line only while it builds the far end, and building it again knocks a Train still out on the line off it.
- Electric Locomotive and charging (#9, ADR 0005 amended): the Electric Locomotive crafts from a Burner Locomotive, four copper ingots and four redstone dust (`#c:ingots/copper`, `#c:dusts/redstone`), and is a Locomotive like the Burner, with its mass and drive, running on a charge of FE instead of fuel: 100 J an FE (`joulesPerFe`), so full power draws 300 FE a tick, up to 10,000,000 FE (`capacity`). It is set on the Rail empty, pulls nothing once empty and brakes down as a Burner out of fuel does, and in a mixed Train each Locomotive pays from its own fuel or charge. Its charge is its record's in the level (`CarRecords.Contents.charge`), so an abstract Train keeps and spends it; breaking the car loses it. Its menu shows a charge gauge where the Burner's shows fuel slots, and its view gives `Capabilities.Energy.ENTITY`, read only, for Jade's own energy display. A **Charger** (copper, redstone and an iron block) places where a hatch does, joining that Station, takes FE from any mod's cable through `Capabilities.Energy.BLOCK`, insert only, at up to 10,000 FE a tick (`chargerRate`) into a 10,000,000 FE buffer (`chargerBuffer`) kept in the level (`LevelChargers`), and charges the Electric Locomotive whose Engine room it stands beside at that rate, of any Train at rest at its Station, manual or automatic, never one held there on its way elsewhere; several beside one Locomotive add up. An automatic Train on a job waits while charge flows and leaves once its Electric Locomotives are full or nothing flows. The **Creative Charger**, in the Works tab with no recipe, never runs out and fills a Locomotive in one tick. The **Burner Generator** (a furnace, iron and copper) burns furnace fuel that leaves nothing behind, from one slot hoppers fill, at 600 kW (`burnerGeneratorKilowatts`) and the Burner Locomotive's joules per burn tick into a 3,000 FE buffer, extract only, and pushes it into the energy handlers on its six sides. The new numbers are under `[electric_locomotive]` in `railworks-server.toml`.
