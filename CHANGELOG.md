# Changelog

Written for Consumers: what a mod building against Railworks can use, or will see change. A published version never changes: a fix is the next patch (`docs/agents/releases.md`).

## Unreleased
- Pylons and Rail: a Pylon crafts from iron and stacks into a column, one Pylon a segment, its Arm on the top segment. Using Rail on one Arm and then another strings a Span costing `ceil(length)` Rail, up to 32 blocks (`spanLengthLimit` in `railworks-server.toml`), at most two to an Arm. Repeating it cuts the Span; breaking a Pylon cuts its Spans, and either refunds the Rail. Spans are saved with the level and a client is sent those in the chunks it loads.
- The Pylon shows Groundworks' Placement Preview, including the segment it would stack onto a column. Placing a new column strings one Span to the nearest Pylon in reach whose Arm has a Span to spare, for Rail from the placer's inventory (creative players too); the preview draws it, faded. Stacking a segment, sneaking, or too little Rail strings nothing.
- A Rail holding the first end of a Span shows a slack Rail from that Arm to the hand, moving to the Pylon aimed at: green where the click would string, orange where it would cut (the Span itself stops drawing) and red where it would be refused.
- The Rail is drawn as an inverted T, its flange resting on the Pylon tops, and a car rides on top of it, straddling the web (ADR 0006).
- Grade and bend limits: a Span climbing steeper than 25% (`spanGradeLimitPercent` in `railworks-server.toml`), or bending through an Arm more than 45 degrees off straight against the Span already there (`spanBendLimit`), is refused and spends nothing, whether strung by hand or by a placed Pylon, which strings to the nearest Arm within both limits instead. The held Rail's slack shows both refusals red, with an action-bar line saying why; the Pylon's Placement Preview draws the Span the limits refused red when it would string nothing. The bend rule is `SpanRules.tooSharp`, judging any two Spans through an Arm. Jade on a Pylon shows the length of each Span at its Arm and, while Rail holds a pending Span, why the Span to that Arm would be refused.
- Burner Locomotive: crafts from iron and a furnace, and a Locomotive in the hand shows Groundworks' Placement Preview of the car riding the Span aimed along (refused off a Span, where the car would sit in a block above the Span, or on a Span another Locomotive holds); using it sets it on the Rail facing the way the player looks. Sneaking at the car, or the inventory key while riding it, opens its menu: the fuel slot (furnace fuel only) over the player's inventory, shift-click moving fuel either way. Fuel in hand still fills the slot, using it otherwise boards it, and sneaking gets off. Forward accelerates the way it faces; back brakes and then reverses, as in Factorio's manual driving. It burns 600 kW of fuel at 2,500 J per burn tick (`joules_per_burn_tick`, as Wireworks' Boiler: coal is 4 MJ), runs on through Arms, and stops at a line's end and when its fuel runs out. A Locomotive is a car of a Train in the rail network model (`rail/Train`, an ordered chain of `rail/Car`s carrying their mass, their fuelled Locomotives' pull and the Spans they stand on), with the entity as its car's view; its top speed (25 blocks a second; Factorio's 72 is allowed), acceleration, braking and friction are in `railworks-server.toml`. Stacking a segment onto a strung column keeps a Locomotive on its Span. Jade shows its fuel and speed.
- Stations: a Station crafts from iron, a Rail and a sign, sits in the Works tab and places beside the Rail or under it, refused off the Rail, where a car passing along its stretch would hit it, or by Rail that is not a Straight leg (ADR 0007). Using it opens its menu, where it is named, and several may share a name. The name is kept with the block, and the level keeps every Station's block and name (`LevelStations`) so a Schedule can find them unloaded. Whether a car's Place is at a Station, within a block either way of the point of the nearest Span by it, is `Station.serves` and `Stations.at` in the rail network model. Jade shows a Station's name, and when no Span is in reach of it.
- Cargo cars: a Cargo car crafts from iron round a chest (`#c:chests/wooden`) and holds a chest's 27 slots, its menu (vanilla's chest) opening when it is used, sneaking or not; Jade shows what it holds. Placing a Cargo car or a Locomotive beside an end of a Train at rest couples it into that Train, facing the way the player looks, so a Train can carry a Locomotive facing out at each end; the Placement Preview draws it there. Shears on a car part its Train at the coupling on the side used, while it stands still. Every car adds weight: a Train accelerates by its fuelled Locomotives' pull over its whole mass, and brakes by its Locomotives' brakes over it, so a Cargo car (1,000 kg, a Locomotive's half) stretches both. A driver drives the Train the way their own Locomotive faces, and the Locomotives facing that way pull. A Locomotive is boarded by using it; a player leading a mob puts the mob in, and a lead used on it leads its mob out. Nobody rides a Cargo car, player or mob. The level now holds its Trains (`rail/Trains`, saved with the Spans), each car's entity keeping only its car's id; a Train holds every Span its cars' bodies stand on, and stops at the Arm of a Span another Train holds. Breaking a car closes its Train up without it and drops what it held; a cut Span drops every car of the Train on it.
- The Rail curves through each Pylon: a Span is drawn as a smooth curve between its two Arms, at each Arm along the bisector of the Spans meeting there and, at a line's end, heading straight at the other Arm; the Pylon's Placement Preview draws the curve the Span would take. Every car rides the curve, its position, heading, pitch and its rider's seat following it, with no jump from one Span onto the next, and a car in the hand aims at the curve. The curve is `rail/RailCurve` (`RailNetwork.curve(span)`, `RailCurve.between` for a Span not yet strung, `Place.pose`), which the renderer and the riding code both ask; `Place.point()` became `Place.point(network)`. Rail cost, the length limit and how far a Train runs along a Span stay the straight distance between the Arms.
- Loading at Stations: a Train at rest with any of its cars by a Station is stopped at it, and every Cargo car of it, wherever along the Train it stands, opens its 27 slots on every side: the Cargo car is a vanilla `WorldlyContainer`, as a chest boat is a `Container`, so a hopper beside the car at its floor pointing at it loads it, a hopper under the Rail pulls from the car above it and unloads it, and Jade shows what it holds as it does a chest boat's. It also exposes NeoForge's `Capabilities.Item.ENTITY_AUTOMATION` on the same rule, for any mod's feeder or pipe that looks for an entity's item handler. Away from that rule its sides hold no slots and it exposes no capability. A Cargo car loads only where it stands on a Straight leg of Rail, every Span under its body in line at both Arms (or ending there) and running along x or z: one on a bend or a diagonal exposes none, shows no spots and takes no hopper, even at a Station, and sneak-using a hopper there says why (`rail/StraightLeg`). A Station is refused, the Placement Preview red, unless every Span its stretch touches is a Straight leg, a stretch reaching over a Pylon included; Jade on a Station whose stretch a Span strung later has bent says so. A Cargo car of a moving Train, or of one stopped away from every Station, exposes none. While a Cargo car's Train is stopped at a Station, a player looking at one of its Cargo cars sees where that car's hoppers go, a yellow box on either side of the car at its floor to fill it and an amber one under the Rail to empty it; other cars draw none. Sneak-using a hopper at a Cargo car stopped at a Station sets it in the yellow spot on the player's side, pointing at the car, standing in the air however high the Train is; it uses up the hopper outside creative, and is refused where the spot is taken or away from a Station. The emptying hopper stands on a block under the Rail, a chest it empties into, say. A spot a Pylon or a Station already fills is not drawn. Which Stations a Train is stopped at is `Stations.stoppedAt`, and where its hoppers go is `HopperSpots`, both in the rail network model; `CargoCarEntity.loading()` is the rule on the server, and `loadingSeen()` what clients are sent of it.
